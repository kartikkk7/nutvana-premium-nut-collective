# Nutvana Premium Nut Collective

ROLE
You are a senior brand-focused web designer and front-end developer. Build a visually stunning, conversion-focused, single-page landing website for "NUTVANA", a premium dry fruits and foxnuts (makhana) brand by Anant Overseas (an India-based export company). The page should feel like a luxury food brand (think premium gifting and gourmet packaging), not a generic template.

BRAND (use the attached logo; do not redraw it)
- Name: NUTVANA. Tagline: "Pure Crunch. Premium Life."
- Logo (Attached) : a green-and-gold "N" made of leaves inside a gold ring, surrounded by almonds, cashews, walnuts, pistachios, hazelnuts and foxnuts, with the wordmark NUT (deep green) + VANA (gold).
- Personality: pure, natural, premium, healthy, trustworthy.
- Products: Premium Foxnuts (Makhana), Almonds, Cashews, Walnuts, Pistachios, Hazelnuts.

GOALS AND AUDIENCE
1. Primary goal: get visitors to request a quote or sample (WhatsApp or the enquiry form).
2. Audiences: (a) importers, distributors, retailers and private-label buyers abroad (UAE, USA, UK, Europe, Africa, Asia); (b) health-conscious premium buyers.
3. Every section should push toward one action: "Request a Free Sample" or "Get a Quote".

DESIGN SYSTEM
- Colours: Deep forest green #0E3B24, rich leaf green #1F7A3A, premium gold #C8961E with highlight #E8B93E, warm ivory #FBF8EF, charcoal text #17261D. Use gold gradients for buttons and accents.
- Typography: an elegant high-contrast serif for headings (Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display), a clean sans for body (Manrope or Inter).
- Style: rich, premium, lots of whitespace, large imagery, soft shadows, gold hairline details, subtle leaf and nut motifs. Alternate light ivory sections with deep green sections for rhythm.
- Buttons: pill-shaped gold gradient primary; outlined secondary. Add smooth hover effects.

IMAGERY (this is critical: the page must be image-rich)
Generate or source high-quality, photorealistic, studio-style food photography, all on-brand (green leaves, gold accents, dark green or ivory backgrounds):
- Hero: a dramatic hero image of nuts and foxnuts splashing or floating, with green leaves and water droplets.
- One premium product shot for each of: foxnuts, almonds, cashews, walnuts, pistachios, hazelnuts.
- A lifestyle image of a bowl of makhana with a cup of tea, and a healthy breakfast bowl with nuts.
- A foxnut pond / lotus plant image (Bihar origin story).
- Packaging mockups: premium pouches with the Nutvana logo, a gift box, and a bulk export carton.
- A world map graphic with shipping route lines.
Use real image files (not emoji, not plain colour blocks). Add descriptive alt text.

PAGE STRUCTURE AND CONTENT

1. Sticky navigation (transparent over the hero, solid after scroll)
Logo | Products | Why Nutvana | Our Story | FAQ | [Get a Quote] button. Mobile hamburger menu with a smooth slide-in.

2. Hero (full-viewport, cinematic)
- Headline: "Pure Crunch. Premium Life."
- Subhead: "Handpicked almonds, cashews, walnuts, pistachios, hazelnuts and light, crisp foxnuts. Premium quality, hygienically packed and delivered worldwide."
- Buttons: [Request a Free Sample] [Explore Products]
- Parallax background image with floating leaf and nut elements that react slightly to mouse movement.
- A small trust row beneath: "Direct from farms | Hand-graded | Export-grade packaging | Shipping worldwide".

3. Animated trust and stats strip
Animated count-up numbers when scrolled into view (use only claims that are safe, for example: "6 premium products", "Shipping to 4+ continents", "FSSAI | APEDA | ISO 22000 certified facilities"), with certification badges.

4. Product showcase (the main section)
Heading: "The Nutvana Collection". Large image cards in a responsive grid with hover zoom, a gold glow and a "Quick View" button that opens a modal with a bigger image, full description, benefits, available pack sizes and a [Request Quote for this product] button, which pre-selects that product in the enquiry form. Add filter tabs: All | Foxnuts | Dry Fruits.
Product content:
- Premium Foxnuts (Makhana) [featured, large card]: "Lotus seeds from the still ponds of Bihar, hand-harvested, sun-dried and popped into light, snow-white puffs. Mild, nutty and satisfyingly crisp. Rich in plant protein and fibre, low in fat and sodium, gluten-free, with a low glycemic index. Enjoy plain, roasted with ghee, in kheer, in curries or as a guilt-free snack." Grades: 4 Suthi, 5 Suthi, 6 Suthi+. Tags: Gluten-free, Vegan, Low GI.
- Premium Almonds: "Plump, crunchy kernels with a sweet, buttery bite. A daily source of vitamin E, healthy fats and protein, perfect for snacking, milk, sweets and baking." 
- Premium Cashews: "Creamy, naturally sweet whole kernels with a uniform pale colour. They make gravies velvety, desserts rich and roasted snacks irresistible."
- Premium Walnuts: "Light golden halves with a mellow, earthy flavour. One of the best plant sources of omega-3 for brain and heart health. Great in breakfast bowls, salads and brownies."
- Premium Pistachios: "Naturally split shells with vivid green, rich, slightly sweet kernels. Perfect for snacking, ice cream, baklava and festive platters."
- Premium Hazelnuts: "Round, aromatic and deeply nutty with a smooth, sweet finish. A chocolatier's favourite, great roasted, ground into spreads or scattered over desserts."

5. Foxnut spotlight (storytelling, scroll-driven)
Split layout with a large pond/lotus image. As the user scrolls, animated steps appear: Grown in still ponds -> Hand-harvested in monsoon -> Sun-dried -> Roasted and popped -> Graded and packed. Follow with an interactive benefits widget (tabs or hover cards): Protein and fibre | Weight-friendly | Heart healthy | Brain support | Low glycemic | Gluten-free.

6. "Why Nutvana" (deep green section)
Six icon cards with subtle hover animation: Sourced at origin | Hygienic processing | Freshness-lock packaging | Certified (FSSAI, APEDA, ISO 22000) | Private label ready | Reliable delivery.

7. Packaging and private label
Showcase pouch, gift box and bulk carton mockups in an image carousel or slider with a before/after style reveal. Copy: "Your brand, our quality. Retail-ready pouches, gifting packs and bulk export cartons, fully customisable." CTA: [Discuss Private Label].

8. Global reach
An interactive world map with animated lines from India to UAE, UK, USA, Africa, Europe and Asia, with a hover tooltip on each region. Heading: "From Indian farms to the world's shelves."

9. Interactive "Find Your Nutvana" mini-quiz
2-3 quick questions (e.g. "I am a: Retailer / Distributor / Importer / Brand owner", "Interested in: Foxnuts / Dry fruits / Both", "Need: Retail packs / Bulk / Private label") that end in a tailored recommendation and a pre-filled enquiry form.

10. Testimonials slider
3 placeholder testimonials from business buyers (clearly easy to replace) with name, company and country, auto-rotating with swipe support.

11. FAQ accordion
- Can I get a sample before ordering? - Do you offer private label? - Which countries do you ship to? - What is your minimum order quantity? - How are the products packed and kept fresh? - What are Suthi grades of makhana?

12. Final CTA banner
Full-width dark green image background: "Ready to taste the Nutvana difference?" with [Request a Free Sample] and [Chat on WhatsApp].

13. Enquiry form
Fields: Name, Company, Email, Phone, Country, Product (multi-select), Quantity, Message. Validate inputs, show a success animation on submit, and send to info@anantoverseas.com (or open a WhatsApp message to +91 7999950100 with the details pre-filled).

14. Footer
Logo, short brand description, quick links, products list, and contact details:

Social icons. Link to anantoverseas.com ("A brand of Anant Overseas").

INTERACTIVITY AND ANIMATION
- Smooth scrolling, scroll-triggered reveals (fade and slide, staggered), parallax on hero and pond images.
- Floating sticky WhatsApp button and a sticky "Get a Quote" bar on mobile.
- Hover effects: image zoom, gold glow and tilt on product cards.
- Count-up numbers, animated map lines, an image lightbox and a testimonial carousel.
- A loading intro: the Nutvana logo fades in with a leaf animation.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
- Fully responsive (mobile-first, check 375px, 768px and 1440px). Fast loading with lazy-loaded, optimised images (WebP).
- Semantic HTML, accessible (alt text, focus states, colour contrast, keyboard navigation).
- SEO: title "Nutvana | Premium Dry Fruits & Foxnuts (Makhana) Exporter from India", meta description, Open Graph tags, and schema.org Organization and Product markup.
- Clean, well-commented, component-based code (React + Tailwind preferred) that is easy to edit.

COPY TONE
Confident, warm, premium and simple. Short sentences. Benefit-led. Avoid unverifiable health claims or fake statistics.

OUTPUT
Deliver a complete, polished, production-ready landing page with all the sections above, real imagery and the interactions described.
